Recently I purchased the Flipper Zero. I've gotten a couple of comments about it: some like "Wow, that's so cool," and others more like "Isn't that dangerous?" or "Isn't that illegal?" My response to both is that it's just a multi-tool that I can use to do different things. I use it for its many useful features. It can copy RFID and NFC tags, as I am doing for a friend. It can act as a UART probe, chip programmer, radio receiver, and even a two-factor authentication tool. These are just some of its capabilities, and you're telling me it's useless or just a toy? Certainly you can use it as that, but you could also use a Swiss Army knife to whittle wood to make toy boats, or a 3D printer to print trinkets. On the other hand, you can use a knife, say, as a dedicated survival tool or as a genuinely useful EDC knife. And 3D printing is a large industry with lots of patents in it, and you can 3D print useful products that improve your life. All that is to say that even though the Flipper Zero is expensive, and might not be worth it for everyone, it is still useful, and in the right hands it can be a practical multi-tool.
